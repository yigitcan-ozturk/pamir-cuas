# TSMS-Drone provenance and licence record

Dataset: Time-Synchronized Multi-Sensor Drone Dataset (TSMS-Drone)

Dataset DOI: 10.25452/figshare.plus.30027313

Associated article DOI: 10.1038/s41597-026-06802-6

The associated Scientific Data Data Availability statement identifies the dataset as publicly accessible under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

Important distinction: the associated journal article has its own publication licence. Dataset artefacts and article content must not be treated as having the same licence.

Required field-validation practice:
1. Preserve dataset attribution and DOI.
2. Preserve original source files as immutable inputs.
3. Record SHA-256 for each acquired source file.
4. Label all PAMIR-CUAS modifications as derived artefacts or controlled perturbations.
5. Do not represent perturbation outputs as observed field events.
